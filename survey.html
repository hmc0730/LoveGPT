Debrief

Thank you for participating

Now that you have completed the survey, we can share what this study was really about.

All three messages you read were generated by AI. Half of participants were told the messages were written by a human; the other half were told they were AI-generated.

We are investigating whether knowing the source of a romantic message changes how sincere it feels, even when the text is identical. Your responses will help us understand how authorship labels shape emotional perception.

If you have any questions, feel free to ask the research team.
